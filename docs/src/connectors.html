Connectors
Parts that line up and stay together
Every cut face gets connectors placed automatically: up to four per face, kept clear of walls, starting at the thickest spot. Each one can be retyped, resized or removed. Clearance is per side and is drawn larger than life here.

Peg 0.15 mm

A solid peg grows out of one part; the other gets a matching hole. Nothing extra to print.

  • Diameter scales with the face, up to 10 mm
  • Peg is clipped so it never pokes through a thin neighbour

Dowel 0.10 mm

Both parts get a hole and Dovetail adds a loose pin to the plates. Good when neither side should have a bump.

  • Pin is 0.6 mm shorter than the two holes
  • Use a printed pin or a cut length of filament or rod

Dovetail 0.25 mm

A flared tenon slides into a slot and can't pull straight out. One per face, centred.

  • Slides vertically when the cut is vertical, so it prints and assembles easily
  • Falls back to a peg where the face is too narrow

Not sure about your printer's tolerance? Export the test coupon first: one strip with six holes from 0.05 to 0.30 mm clearance plus the matching key. Count the notches above the hole that fits best and type that value in. It takes a few minutes to print and saves reprinting a big part.
